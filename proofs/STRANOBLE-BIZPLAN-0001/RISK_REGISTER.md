## Risk Categories

### 1. MARKET & COMPETITIVE RISKS

#### R-MKT-001: Market Size Insufficient

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Total addressable market smaller than projected |
| **Likelihood** | Low |
| **Impact** | High |
| **Category** | Market |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Difficulty finding qualified prospects
- Low response rates to outreach
- Price resistance across segments

**Mitigation:**
- Conservative SOM projections (1-2% of SAM)
- Focus on proven pain points with quantifiable impact
- Geographic expansion if local market insufficient

**Contingency:**
- Pivot to adjacent markets (e-commerce, professional services)
- Adjust pricing for broader accessibility

---

#### R-MKT-002: Competitive Pressure Intensifies

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Existing competitors or new entrants target same segment |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Competitive |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Agencies lowering prices
- New productized services entering market
- DIY platforms adding managed services

**Mitigation:**
- Differentiated positioning (operational focus, outcomes)
- Build case study library as proof moat
- Develop proprietary frameworks and tools

**Contingency:**
- Specialize further into specific verticals
- Increase referral partner development

---

#### R-MKT-003: Price Sensitivity Higher Than Expected

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Target customers unwilling to pay current price points |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Market |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Low proposal conversion rates
- Frequent price negotiation requests
- Prospects choosing DIY alternatives

**Mitigation:**
- Clear ROI demonstration in proposals
- Entry-tier offer (Lead Rescue) for quick wins
- Outcome-based pricing tied to value delivered

**Contingency:**
- Develop lower-tier productized offering
- Payment plans for larger engagements

---

#### R-MKT-004: Economic Downturn

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Recession reduces small business spending |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Market |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Macroeconomic indicators weakening
- Client budget cuts or delays
- Increased churn in retainers

**Mitigation:**
- Position as essential (revenue capture, not optional)
- Lean cost structure (variable costs tied to delivery)
- Maintain cash reserve (3-6 months)

**Contingency:**
- Emphasize cost savings and efficiency gains
- Offer flexible payment terms

### 2. OPERATIONAL RISKS

#### R-OPS-001: Founder Dependency (Key Person Risk)

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Business cannot operate without founder involvement |
| **Likelihood** | High |
| **Impact** | High |
| **Category** | Operational |
| **Status** | Active Mitigation |

**Indicators:**
- All client relationships through founder
- No documented processes
- Unable to take time off

**Mitigation:**
- Document all processes in SOP library
- Build specialist contractor bench
- Create client handoff protocols
- Systematize repeatable deliverables

**Contingency:**
- Business interruption insurance
- Emergency contractor network

---

#### R-OPS-002: Delivery Capacity Overload

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Sales outpace delivery capacity, quality suffers |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | High |
| **Category** | Operational |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Project delays increasing
- Quality complaints
- Working excessive hours

**Mitigation:**
- Capped scope in all offerings
- Productized templates reduce custom work
- Pre-qualified contractor bench
- Pipeline visibility (know what's coming)

**Contingency:**
- Pause sales temporarily
- Bring in contractors for overflow
- Extend timelines with client communication

---

#### R-OPS-003: Quality Inconsistency

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Deliverable quality varies across engagements |
| **Likelihood** | Low |
| **Impact** | High |
| **Category** | Operational |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Client complaints or revision requests
- Different outcomes across similar projects
- Contractor work not meeting standards

**Mitigation:**
- Standardized frameworks and checklists
- QA review before delivery
- Clear acceptance criteria in SOPs
- Contractor quality standards documented

**Contingency:**
- Immediate remediation for quality issues
- Refund policy for unresolvable problems

---

#### R-OPS-004: Tool/Platform Dependency

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Critical tools change pricing, features, or availability |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Low-Medium |
| **Category** | Operational |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Vendor acquisition or shutdown announcements
- Significant pricing changes
- Feature deprecation

**Mitigation:**
- Multi-platform capability (not locked to one vendor)
- Skills transferable across similar tools
- Avoid deep lock-in to any single platform

**Contingency:**
- Migrate clients to alternative platforms
- Adjust pricing if tool costs increase significantly

### 3. FINANCIAL RISKS

#### R-FIN-001: Slow Sales Cycle

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Time from lead to close longer than projected |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Financial |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Deals stalling in pipeline
- Extended decision timelines
- Multiple stakeholder involvement

**Mitigation:**
- Entry-tier offer (Lead Rescue) for fast decisions
- Clear, fixed-scope proposals
- Urgency-building in sales process

**Contingency:**
- Adjust cash flow projections
- Increase outreach volume to compensate

---

#### R-FIN-002: Collection/Payment Issues

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Clients delay or fail to pay invoices |
| **Likelihood** | Low |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Financial |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Late payments increasing
- Disputed invoices
- Client financial distress

**Mitigation:**
- Deposits required before work begins (typically 50%)
- Clear payment terms in contracts
- Milestone-based billing for larger projects
- Credit check for larger engagements

**Contingency:**
- Collections process documented
- Legal templates for enforcement

---

| **Likelihood** | Low |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Financial |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**

- Delayed vendor payments
- Increased credit usage

### 4. EXECUTION RISKS

#### R-EXE-001: Client Trust/Proof Insufficient

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Cannot demonstrate credibility to prospects |
| **Likelihood** | Medium (early stage) |
| **Impact** | High |
| **Category** | Execution |
| **Status** | Active Mitigation |

**Indicators:**
- Low proposal conversion
- Requests for references not satisfied
- Competitor comparisons unfavorable

**Mitigation:**
- Short case studies with specific metrics
- Clear scope and deliverables in proposals
- Rapid first wins to build proof
- Testimonials collected systematically

**Contingency:**
- Discounted pilot engagements to build proof
- Partner with established referrers

---

#### R-EXE-002: Scope Creep

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Description** | Project scope expands beyond agreed terms |
| **Likelihood** | Medium |
| **Impact** | Medium |
| **Category** | Execution |
| **Status** | Monitored |

**Indicators:**
- Client requests outside original scope
- Project timelines extending
- Margin compression on engagements

**Mitigation:**

- Documented change request process
