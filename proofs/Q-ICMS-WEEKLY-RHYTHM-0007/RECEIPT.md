## Success Metrics Established

The rhythm includes built-in tracking:

1. **Staleness**: % of projects with updates <7 days old
   - Target: >90%
   - Measured: Monthly

2. **Invoice Velocity**: Average days from invoice sent to payment
   - Target: <14 days
   - Measured: Weekly (Wednesday review)

3. **Action Completion**: % of next actions completed on time
   - Target: >85%
   - Measured: Weekly (Friday review)

4. **Decision Coverage**: Number of major decisions logged per month
   - Target: Track trend
   - Measured: Monthly

## Risk Mitigation

**Risk**: Team skips weeks, rhythm degrades
**Mitigation**:
- Miss once = email update required
- Miss twice = calendar block + pairing session
- Built into rhythm document

**Risk**: Sessions run long, unsustainable
**Mitigation**:
- Explicit time boxes in checklist
- "Don't solve problems during review" rule
- Timer recommendations

**Risk**: Q-ICMS not trusted/used
**Mitigation**:
- "Single source of truth" principle
- Success metrics track data quality
- Monthly audits identify gaps
