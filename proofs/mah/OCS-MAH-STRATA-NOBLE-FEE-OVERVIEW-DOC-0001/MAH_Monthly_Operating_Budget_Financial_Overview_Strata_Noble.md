### 1. Overview

The monthly operating budget for Ms. Audrey's House is **$1,500 per month**.

This budget covers the ongoing business operations, platform management, reporting, coordination, and operational support required to maintain and improve the Ms. Audrey's House online business.

The budget is separate from direct business expenses such as product costs, Shopify fees, shipping, refunds, payment processing, paid advertising, taxes, and third-party software expenses.

### 2. Purpose of the monthly operating budget

The purpose of the monthly operating budget is to provide Ms. Audrey's House with a managed operating structure that supports the day-to-day function of the business.

This includes maintaining visibility into store activity, organizing business information, monitoring orders, supporting product and supplier updates, coordinating approvals, and helping the business operate with clearer systems and communication.

The monthly budget is intended to cover the management layer around the business, not the direct cost of goods, advertising, fulfillment, or external vendor fees.

### 3. Services covered by the monthly budget

#### 3.1 Store and platform management

Strata Noble provides ongoing support for the digital store environment connected to Ms. Audrey's House.

This includes:

- Monitoring the Shopify store  
- Reviewing store activity  
- Supporting product and catalog updates  
- Checking for store, checkout, or customer-facing issues  
- Helping maintain the structure of the online storefront  
- Supporting updates to product information as needed  

**Allocated monthly value:** $350

#### 3.2 Operations support

Strata Noble provides operational support to help keep the business organized and functional.

This includes:

- Monitoring order activity  
- Reviewing supplier and product information  
- Supporting intake of business and vendor details  
- Tracking open operational items  
- Coordinating updates related to products, promotions, and business activity  
- Helping reduce confusion around what needs attention  

**Allocated monthly value:** $350

#### 3.3 Reporting and business visibility

Strata Noble provides visibility into store activity and business status so the business owner can understand what is happening without needing to manage every system directly.

This includes:

- Weekly operating summaries  
- Order and revenue review  
- Open issue tracking  
- Product and promotion status updates  
- Basic performance observations  
- Communication around items requiring attention or approval  

**Allocated monthly value:** $250

#### 3.4 Communication and coordination

Strata Noble maintains the communication rhythm required to support business operations.

This includes:

- Operational updates  
- Approval requests when business decisions are needed  
- Follow-up on open questions  
- Coordination around changes, promotions, or store-related issues  
- Maintaining organized communication around business activity  

**Allocated monthly value:** $250

#### 3.5 Business improvement and process cleanup

Strata Noble supports ongoing improvement of the business structure as Ms. Audrey's House grows.

This includes:

- Organizing supplier and product information  
- Cleaning up operational processes  
- Improving how business information is collected  
- Creating clearer systems for tracking work  
- Identifying areas where the business can operate more efficiently  
- Supporting better decision-making through structure and visibility  

**Allocated monthly value:** $300

### 5. Expenses not included in the monthly operating budget

The monthly operating budget does not include direct business expenses incurred by Ms. Audrey's House.

These expenses remain separate from Strata Noble's operating budget and may be paid directly by Ms. Audrey's House or reimbursed if paid on behalf of the business.

Excluded expenses include, but are not limited to:

- Shopify subscription fees  
- Shopify transaction or payment processing fees  
- Product costs  
- Supplier costs  
- Shipping and fulfillment costs  
- Return shipping  
- Refunds  
- Chargebacks  
- Domain registration or renewal fees  
- Paid advertising spend  
- Paid Shopify apps or third-party software  
- Taxes  
- Accounting services  
- Legal services  
- Inventory purchases  
- Packaging or branded material costs  
- Any vendor, supplier, or platform fees outside the agreed operating scope  

### 6. Approval of additional costs

Any new recurring software, platform, supplier, advertising, or vendor expense outside the monthly operating budget should be reviewed before being added to the business.

Any expense above an agreed approval threshold should require written approval before being incurred.

**Recommended approval threshold:** Any new cost above **$50 per month** or any one-time cost above **$100** should require written approval.

### 7. Financial transparency statement

The **$1,500** monthly operating budget represents the cost of ongoing management, coordination, reporting, and operational support for Ms. Audrey's House.

This budget is designed to provide the business with a consistent operating structure and reduce the burden of managing the store, systems, communication, and business activity without support.

Direct business expenses remain separate so that the cost of operating the business is clear, traceable, and transparent.

### 8. Summary

Ms. Audrey's House pays Strata Noble **$1,500 per month** for operational management and business support.

The budget covers the work required to help keep the online business organized, monitored, reported, supported, and improved over time.

The budget does not cover the direct costs of running the business, such as products, shipping, Shopify fees, advertising, refunds, taxes, or third-party vendor expenses.

Those expenses remain separate and should be reviewed, approved, and tracked independently from the monthly operating budget.
