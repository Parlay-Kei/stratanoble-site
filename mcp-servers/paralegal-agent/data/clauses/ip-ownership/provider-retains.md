## Intellectual Property Ownership

**Provider Pre-existing IP**. Provider retains all ownership rights in and to any pre-existing intellectual property, including but not limited to frameworks, methodologies, tools, libraries, components, and know-how that existed prior to this Agreement or were developed independently of work performed under this Agreement ("Provider IP").

**License Grant**. Provider grants Client a perpetual, non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-free license to use Provider IP solely as incorporated into the Deliverables and solely for Client's internal business purposes. Client may not extract, modify, or use Provider IP separately from the Deliverables without Provider's prior written consent.

**Client Deliverables**. Subject to Provider's retention of Provider IP, Client shall own all right, title, and interest in and to the custom work product created specifically for Client under this Agreement ("Deliverables"), including custom code, designs, documentation, and materials that do not constitute Provider IP.
