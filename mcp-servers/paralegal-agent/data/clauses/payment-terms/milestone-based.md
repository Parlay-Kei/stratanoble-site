## Payment Terms

**Fees**. Client shall pay Provider the fees set forth in the applicable Statement of Work.

**Deposit**. Client shall pay a non-refundable deposit of {{deposit_percent}}% of the total project fee upon execution of the Statement of Work. The deposit reserves Provider's resources and covers initial project setup costs.

**Milestone Payments**. The remaining fees shall be paid according to the milestone schedule set forth in the Statement of Work. Provider shall submit an invoice upon completion of each milestone. Each invoice shall describe the completed work and deliverables.

**Payment Due Date**. All invoices are due and payable within {{net_days}} days of the invoice date, unless otherwise specified in the Statement of Work.

**Late Payments**. Payments not received by the due date are subject to a late fee of {{late_fee_percent}}% per month (or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less). Provider may suspend performance of services if payment is more than {{suspension_days}} days overdue, without penalty or liability.

**Taxes**. All fees are exclusive of applicable taxes. Client shall pay or reimburse Provider for all sales, use, value-added, and other taxes (excluding taxes based on Provider's net income) arising from the services or this Agreement.

**Expenses**. Client shall reimburse Provider for reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket expenses incurred in performing services, including travel, accommodation, and third-party costs, with supporting documentation.
