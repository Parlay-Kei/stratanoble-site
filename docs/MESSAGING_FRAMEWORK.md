## 1. Core Identity

### What Is StrataNoble?

**Primary Statement (Use in Hero, About sections):**
> StrataNoble helps everyday operators turn heavy, chaotic businesses into something they can actually manage. We work with solo and small business owners—practices, shops, studios, consultancies—who are carrying the whole thing on their backs and need clearer decisions, calmer operations, and more honest plans.

**Short Form (Use in meta descriptions, social):**
> A platform for everyday entrepreneurs who want clear strategy, real market intelligence, and AI tools that turn scattered ideas into sustainable income.

### What We Are NOT

- A get-rich-quick scheme
- For VC-backed startups
- Another generic "business support" platform
- A replacement for doing the work

## 4. Target Audience

### Primary Segments

**Local Practices & Services**
- Medical offices, dental, salons, gyms, repair shops

**Solo & Micro-Firms**
- Consultants, designers, bookkeepers, coaches

**Small Teams Without Back Office**
- Agencies, boutiques, specialist shops

### Audience Characteristics

People who are:
- Sitting on ideas and unsure which one to move on first
- Already running a small business and tired of guessing the next move
- Balancing real life—family, work, bills—while trying to grow something
- Looking for a clear plan built on data, not hype
