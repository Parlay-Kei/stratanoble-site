# Weekly LinkedIn Operating Rhythm

**Mission ID:** ANX-LINKEDIN-MGMT-0001  
**Goal:** Keep posting consistent, reduce Steve's manual effort, and tie content to business conversations.

## Weekly Cadence (Owner: Agent System)

### Monday - Publish + Engage
- Publish scheduled post (after approval)
- Engage with first 10 comments within 24 hours
- Send 2-3 context-aware DMs from engaged operators

### Tuesday - Comment Pipeline
- Publish no post
- Leave 5 strategic comments on target audience posts
- Capture 3 content ideas from market conversations

### Wednesday - Publish + Qualification
- Publish scheduled post (after approval)
- Respond to comments, identify qualified conversations
- Update tracker with early engagement data

### Thursday - Profile and Queue Maintenance
- Leave 5 strategic comments
- Refresh upcoming post hooks and CTAs
- Update profile featured content if needed

### Friday - Publish + Offer Signal
- Publish offer-oriented or diagnostic-oriented post
- Follow up with warm commenters and DMs
- Capture conversion signals in tracker

### Saturday - Light Touch
- Reply to pending comments/messages
- Mark completed actions and clean queue

### Sunday - Weekly Review
- Record weekly performance notes
- Select top-performing hook and CTA pattern
- Prep next week's 3-post lineup for approval

## Monthly Review Rhythm

Run on final 2 days of each month:

1. Review top 3 posts by meaningful conversations (not vanity likes)
2. Audit pillar balance and rebalance upcoming month
3. Identify repeated objections/questions to turn into post topics
4. Prune weak CTAs and rewrite for next cycle
5. Archive month receipts and create next month queue

## "Done" Definition Each Week

- 3 approved posts published
- At least 20 strategic comments delivered
- Weekly notes written
- All published posts have receipts
- Follow-up list captured for next week
