# LinkedIn Content Pillars

**Mission ID:** ANX-LINKEDIN-MGMT-0001

## Pillar Set

1. Operational mistakes service businesses keep repeating
2. Revenue leakage from poor follow-up and unclear workflows
3. Founder/operator lessons from building Strata Noble, Q Suite, and Direct Cuts
4. Practical automation without hype
5. Business infrastructure for small operators
6. Behind-the-scenes build notes
7. Client education posts
8. Contrarian takes on systems, process, and AI tools
9. Personal authority posts tied to Steve's journey
10. Offer-driven posts leading to diagnostics or consultations

## Pillar Outcomes

- Build practical authority (not influencer authority)
- Generate qualified business conversations
- Teach buyers how to self-diagnose operational gaps
- Move readers toward diagnostics/consulting conversations

## Pillar-to-Format Map

- **Story post:** Pillars 3, 6, 9
- **List/framework post:** Pillars 1, 2, 4, 5, 7
- **Contrarian post:** Pillars 8, 4
- **Offer post:** Pillars 10, 2, 5
- **Comment engagement:** Pillars 1, 2, 4, 8

## Tone Checklist Per Post

- Sounds like Steve speaking to another operator
- Includes at least one concrete operating detail
- Avoids jargon pileups and generic growth platitudes
- Ends with a conversation CTA instead of hard-sell language

## Prohibited or Restricted Content

- No ProofLoop promotion in public posts
- No private/internal-only terms unless Steve approves
- No fabricated metrics or implied guarantees
- No broad "works for everyone" claims
