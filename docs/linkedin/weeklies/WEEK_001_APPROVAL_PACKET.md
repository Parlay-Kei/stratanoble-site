- **Suggested Posting Day:** Monday
- **CTA / Conversation Prompt:** `Comment "intake" for the checklist.`
- **Final LinkedIn-Ready Copy:**

Most service businesses do not have a lead problem.  
They have an intake problem.

Leads come in. Nobody owns first response.  
Follow-up is random. Notes are scattered.  
Then people say demand is weak.

If your first 24 hours are sloppy, revenue leaks before sales even starts.

Simple fix:
1) One intake path

No fancy stack required. Just operational discipline.
