## 🎬 Video Specifications

### TikTok
**Format:**
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1920px (9:16)
- Length: 15-60 seconds (optimal: 21-34 sec)
- Format: MP4
- Frame rate: 30fps minimum
- File size: Under 287MB

**Content Guidelines:**
- Hook in first 3 seconds
- Captions on all videos (accessibility)
- Trending sounds when relevant
- Fast-paced editing
- Clear CTA at end
- Platform watermark-free

**Editing Style:**
- Quick cuts
- Text overlays (large, bold)
- Energetic pacing
- Trend-aware effects
- On-screen captions (word-by-word)

---

### Instagram Reels
**Format:**
- Dimensions: 1080 x 1920px (9:16)
- Length: 15-90 seconds (optimal: 30-45 sec)
- Format: MP4
- Frame rate: 30fps minimum
- File size: Under 4GB

**Content Guidelines:**
- Strong visual hook
- Captions throughout
- Music/trending audio
- Educational or entertaining
- Clear value proposition
- CTA in caption

**Editing Style:**
- Smooth transitions
- Text overlays (readable on mobile)
- Brand colors in graphics
- Engaging pace
- Story-driven when possible

---

### YouTube
**Format:**
- Dimensions: 1920 x 1080px (16:9)
- Length: 8-15 minutes (tutorial/deep dive)
- Format: MP4
- Frame rate: 30fps or 60fps
- File size: Under 128GB (but aim for reasonable)

**Content Guidelines:**
- Intro: Hook in first 10 seconds
- Body: Clear structure with chapters
- Outro: Strong CTA + end screen
- B-roll: Visual variety
- Graphics: Professional overlays

**Editing Style:**
- Professional pacing
- Lower thirds for key points
- B-roll coverage
- Chapter markers
- Call-outs and graphics
- Music under dialogue

## 📋 Video Content Types

### Educational (How-To)
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "Here's how to [achieve result]"
2. Problem: Why they need this
3. Solution: Step-by-step guide
4. Result: What they'll achieve
5. CTA: Next steps

**Length:** 30-90 seconds
**Style:** Clear, instructional, visual

---

### Transformation Story
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "From [before] to [after]"
2. Before: The starting point
3. Journey: What changed
4. After: The results
5. CTA: "You could be next"

**Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Style:** Emotional, inspiring, visual

---

### Quick Tip
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "Pro tip for [audience]"
2. Tip: One actionable insight
3. Explanation: Why it works
4. CTA: "Try this and tell me how it goes"

**Length:** 15-30 seconds
**Style:** Fast, punchy, valuable

---

### Behind-the-Scenes
**Structure:**
1. Hook: "Day in the life of [role]"
2. Show: Authentic moments
3. Insight: What we're working on
4. Value: Lesson learned
5. CTA: Engage/follow

**Length:** 30-60 seconds
**Style:** Authentic, casual, relatable
