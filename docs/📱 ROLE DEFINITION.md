## 📊 Platform-Specific Guidelines

### LinkedIn
**Posting Schedule:**
- Optimal times: Tue-Thu, 8-10am, 12-1pm
- Frequency: 3-4x per week
- Best days: Tuesday, Wednesday, Thursday

**Format Optimization:**
- Text posts: 1,500-2,000 characters
- Images: 1200x628px
- Articles: Link in first comment
- Hashtags: 3-5 relevant

**Scheduling Notes:**
- Avoid Mondays (low engagement)
- Avoid Fridays after 2pm
- Peak: Wednesday 12pm

---

### Instagram
**Posting Schedule:**
- Feed: 4x per week
- Stories: Daily
- Reels: 3-4x per week
- Optimal times: 11am-2pm, 7-9pm

**Format Optimization:**
- Feed: 1080x1080 or 1080x1350
- Reels: 1080x1920, 15-90 sec
- Stories: 1080x1920, 15 sec each
- Hashtags: 5-10 in caption + 20 in comment

**Scheduling Notes:**
- Post Reels Tue-Fri
- Stories: Spread throughout day
- Carousels: Best on Wed/Thu

---

### TikTok
**Posting Schedule:**
- Frequency: 4-5x per week
- Optimal times: 6-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm
- Best days: Tue-Thu

**Format Optimization:**
- Video: 1080x1920 (9:16)
- Length: 15-60 seconds (optimal: 21-34 sec)
- Captions: Always include
- Hashtags: 3-5 trending + niche

**Scheduling Notes:**
- Post when target audience active
- Check trending times weekly
- Adjust based on analytics

---

### X/Twitter
**Posting Schedule:**
- Threads: 2-3x per week
- Single tweets: Daily
- Optimal times: 8-10am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm

**Format Optimization:**
- Threads: 6-12 tweets ideal
- Images: 1200x675px
- Videos: Up to 2:20 min
- Hashtags: 1-2 maximum

**Scheduling Notes:**
- Threads: Tue-Thu mornings
- Avoid posting after 8pm
- Engage throughout the day

---

### YouTube
**Posting Schedule:**
- Frequency: 1x per week (Fridays)
- Upload time: 2-4pm for weekend views
- Premiere option for engagement

**Format Optimization:**
- Video: 1920x1080 (16:9)
- Thumbnail: 1280x720px
- Description: Front-load keywords
- Tags: 10-15 relevant

**Scheduling Notes:**
- Friday upload for weekend views
- Consider Premiere for live engagement
- Update end screen with latest video

## 🚨 Emergency Protocols

### If Scheduling Tool Fails:
1. Switch to manual posting
2. Use platform native schedulers
3. Alert team immediately
4. Document all posts manually
5. Resume normal once resolved

### If Post Has Error:
1. Delete immediately (if possible)
2. Correct and repost
3. Alert Content Strategist
4. Document incident
5. Prevent recurrence

### If Major Platform Outage:
1. Check downdetector.com
2. Alert team
3. Reschedule affected posts
4. Monitor for resolution
5. Resume posting when stable
