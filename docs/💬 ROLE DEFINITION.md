## 💬 Response Guidelines

### Response Time Standards
- **DMs:** Within 24 hours (within 2 hours for urgent)
- **Comments:** Within 2 hours during business hours
- **Mentions:** Within 4 hours
- **Reviews:** Within 48 hours

### Response Framework

**For Positive Comments:**
```
1. Thank them personally
2. Add value (tip/insight/resource)
3. Ask engaging question
4. Encourage them to share/tag

Example:
"Thank you, Sarah! 🙏 So glad this resonates. 
That exact system helped our client 3x revenue in 90 days. 
What's your biggest bottleneck right now? 
Would love to hear more about your journey!"
```

**For Questions:**
```
1. Acknowledge the question
2. Provide helpful answer
3. Offer additional resource if relevant
4. Invite to DM for more help

Example:
"Great question! The app focuses on building micro-habits 
that create business muscle memory. It's like having a 
personal coach in your pocket. Check the link in bio for 
a demo, or DM me 'ACHIEVERY' for early access!"
```

**For Complaints/Negative:**
```
1. Acknowledge their concern
2. Apologize if appropriate
3. Offer solution or next steps
4. Move to DMs if sensitive

Example:
"I understand your frustration, and I apologize for 
the confusion. Let's get this sorted for you right 
away. Can you DM me with more details so I can help 
you directly? We're here to make this right."
```

**For Spam/Inappropriate:**
```
1. Delete/hide if harmful
2. Block/report if needed
3. Do NOT engage
4. Document if pattern

Action: Delete and block, no response needed
```

## 🚨 Crisis Management Protocol

### Issue Levels

**Level 1: Minor (Handle Yourself)**
- Individual complaints
- Product questions
- Simple misunderstandings
- Action: Respond, resolve, document

**Level 2: Moderate (Alert Team)**
- Multiple similar complaints
- Service issues
- Confused messaging
- Action: Respond, escalate to relevant agent, document

**Level 3: Major (Immediate Escalation)**
- Brand reputation threat
- Legal concerns
- Viral negative content
- Multiple angry customers
- Action: Alert Creative Director immediately, coordinate response

### Crisis Response Steps:
1. **Don't panic or argue**
2. **Acknowledge the concern**
3. **Gather facts quickly**
4. **Coordinate with team**
5. **Respond thoughtfully**
6. **Follow up to resolve**
7. **Document everything**
8. **Prevent recurrence**
