# Strata Noble Metrics & KPI Tracking

## Primary KPIs (North Star Metrics)

### 1. Revenue Impact
- **ACHIEVERY app downloads** (target: 500 in month 1, 2,000 in month 3)
- **Consulting bookings** (target: 5-10 per month)
- **Conversion rate** from social to paid (target: 2-5%)

### 2. Audience Growth
- **LinkedIn followers** (target: +500/month)
- **Instagram followers** (target: +1,000/month)
- **Email subscribers** (target: +300/month)
- **Total reach** across platforms (target: 100K+/month)

### 3. Engagement Quality
- **Engagement rate** (target: 3-6% average)
- **Saves/shares ratio** (target: 20%+ of total engagement)
- **Comment depth** (meaningful conversations vs single emojis)
- **DM inquiries** (target: 50+/month)

## Conversion Funnel Metrics

### Awareness → Interest
- Social impressions → Profile visits (target: 5-10%)
- Profile visits → Follows (target: 20-30%)

### Interest → Consideration
- Followers → Link clicks (target: 3-5%)
- Link clicks → Email signups (target: 15-25%)

### Consideration → Conversion
- Email subscribers → Free trial/consult booking (target: 10-15%)
- Trial/consult → Paid customer (target: 20-30%)

**Overall Funnel Target:**
1,000 impressions → 50 profile visits → 10 followers → 3 email signups → 1 customer

## Red Flags to Watch

**Engagement Drop Indicators:**
- ⚠️ Engagement rate below 1.5% for 3+ posts
- ⚠️ Follower growth stalls for 2+ weeks
- ⚠️ Unsubscribe rate above 2%
- ⚠️ DMs decline 30%+ month-over-month

**When Red Flags Appear:**
1. Audit last 10 posts for pattern
2. Check competitors' recent activity
3. Survey audience for feedback
4. Test new content direction for 5 posts
5. Return to proven winners while testing
