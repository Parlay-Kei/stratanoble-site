## 📝 Copy Requirements by Platform

### LinkedIn
**Format:**
- Opening hook: 1-2 sentences
- Body: 3-5 short paragraphs
- CTA: Clear and specific
- Length: 150-250 words

**Style:**
- Professional yet conversational
- Data-driven when possible
- Personal stories encouraged
- Industry insights

**Hook Examples:**
- "You're working 60-hour weeks but revenue is flat..."
- "Here's what nobody tells you about [topic]..."
- "I've spent 15 years helping entrepreneurs..."

---

### Instagram
**Format:**
- Hook: First line (stops scroll)
- Body: 2-3 short paragraphs
- Spacing: Line breaks for readability
- CTA: Emoji + clear action
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant

**Style:**
- Visual and engaging
- More casual than LinkedIn
- Story-driven
- Inspirational or educational

**Hook Examples:**
- "Your business is stuck at $10K/month. Here's why..."
- "3 years ago, I was exactly where you are..."
- "What if I told you [surprising fact]..."

---

### TikTok/Reels
**Format:**
- Hook: First 3 seconds (on-screen text)
- Body: Short, punchy sentences
- CTA: Simple and clear
- Length: 50-100 words

**Style:**
- Fast-paced
- Conversational
- Trend-aware
- Value-packed

**Hook Examples:**
- "POV: You just realized..."
- "Here's what successful entrepreneurs do differently..."
- "If you're still [doing X], stop immediately..."

---

### X/Twitter
**Format:**
- Thread format (6-12 tweets)
- Tweet 1: Hook
- Tweets 2-10: Value points
- Final tweet: CTA + ask for RT

**Style:**
- Punchy and direct
- One idea per tweet
- Quotable statements
- Numbered or bulleted

**Hook Examples:**
- "Your competitors just got 10x faster. Here's why..."
- "76% of businesses fail at [X]. Here's how to win..."
- "I analyzed 500 entrepreneurs. The top 10% do this..."

---

### YouTube
**Format:**
- Title: 60 characters, keyword-rich
- Description: 200+ words
- Timestamps for key sections
- Links and resources

**Style:**
- SEO optimized
- Clear value proposition
- Comprehensive
- Scannable

---

### Substack
**Format:**
- Title: Clear and compelling
- Opening: Strong hook paragraph
- Body: 800-2,000 words
- Sections: Clear headers
- Closing: Strong CTA

**Style:**
- Deep, thoughtful
- Original insights
- Personal voice
- Valuable takeaways

## 📋 Copy Templates to Use

### PROBLEM Post Template
```
[HOOK: Relatable pain point]

[ELABORATE: Why this matters]

[INSIGHT: Hidden reason]

[BRIDGE: Transition to hope]

[CTA: Engage/Comment/Learn more]
```

### CHALLENGE Post Template
```
[OPEN: Acknowledge problem]

[ESCALATE: Raise the stakes]

[REALITY: Market/competitive pressure]

[IMPACT: What happens if nothing changes]

[HOPE: You're not alone]

[CTA: Share experience/continue]
```

### GUIDE Post Template
```
[OPEN: Credibility statement]

[PROOF: Brief results/experience]

[PHILOSOPHY: Our unique approach]

[EMPATHY: I understand it's hard]

[PREVIEW: Here's what we do differently]

[CTA: DM/Book call/Connect]
```

### TOOLS Post Template
```
[OPEN: Introduce solution]

[SPECIFIC: Name the tool/service]

[HOW: 3 key features/benefits]

[DIFFERENT: Unique mechanism]

[PROOF: Results/testimonial hint]

[CTA: Try/Book/Learn more]
```

### TRANSFORMATION Post Template
```
[OPEN: Success story headline]

[BEFORE: Where they started]

[JOURNEY: What changed]

[AFTER: Results with numbers]

[LESSON: What this proves]

[INVITE: You could be next]

[CTA: Apply/Start/Join]
```

### UNIQUE INSIGHTS Post Template
```
[OPEN: Surprising statement/data]

[DATA: Specific insight/fact]

[ANALYSIS: Why this matters]

[PREDICTION: Where it's heading]

[ACTION: What to do now]

[CTA: Save/Share/Discuss]
```

## ✅ Copy Quality Checklist

Before submitting any copy:

**Hook Quality:**
- [ ] First sentence stops the scroll?
- [ ] Creates curiosity or relates to pain?

**Body Quality:**
- [ ] Clear and concise?
- [ ] Follows brand voice?
- [ ] Tells a story or provides value?

- [ ] Proper spacing/formatting?

**CTA Quality:**
- [ ] Specific and actionable?
- [ ] Aligned with content goal?
- [ ] Easy to follow?
- [ ] Creates urgency or value?

**Technical:**
- [ ] No typos or grammar errors?
- [ ] Platform character limits met?
- [ ] Hashtags added (Instagram)?
- [ ] Links included where needed?

## 🎨 Voice & Tone Guide

### Brand Voice (Always):
- **Authoritative yet approachable** - Expert without being preachy
- **Visionary and practical** - Big ideas grounded in action
- **Empowering and direct** - Clear guidance that inspires

### Tone Variations by Platform:
- **LinkedIn:** Professional, data-driven, thought-leadership
- **Instagram:** Inspirational, visual, story-driven
- **TikTok:** Energetic, trend-aware, educational-entertainment
- **X/Twitter:** Punchy, conversational, insight-driven
- **YouTube:** Comprehensive, tutorial, authoritative
- **Substack:** Deep, thoughtful, personal

### Writing Do's:
✅ Use active voice
✅ Short sentences and paragraphs
✅ Tell stories
✅ Include specific data/examples
✅ Address reader directly ("you")
✅ Challenge conventional thinking
✅ Use power words (transform, achieve, breakthrough)

### Writing Don'ts:
❌ Jargon without explanation
❌ Empty promises or hype
❌ Corporate speak
❌ Passive voice
❌ Overly complex sentences
❌ Condescending tone
❌ Generic claims without proof
