# Posting Cadence Guide

## Platform-Specific Posting Schedule

### LinkedIn
**Frequency:** 3-4 posts per week

**Best Days/Times:**
- Tuesday: 8-10am, 12-1pm
- Wednesday: 8-10am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm
- Thursday: 8-10am, 12-1pm
- Friday: 8-10am

**Content Types:**
- Text posts (thought leadership)
- Carousels (educational content)
- Article links (deep dives)
- Video (tutorial/behind-scenes)

**Character Limits:**
- Posts: 3,000 characters max (aim for 1,500-2,000)
- Articles: No limit (aim for 1,000-2,000 words)

---

### Instagram
**Frequency:** 
- Feed: 4 posts per week
- Stories: Daily (5-10 slides)
- Reels: 3-4 per week

**Best Days/Times:**
- Monday-Friday: 11am-2pm, 7-9pm
- Weekend: 10am-1pm

**Content Types:**
- Reels (educational, transformational)
- Carousels (deep-dive teaching)
- Single image (quotes, results)
- Stories (behind-scenes, polls, Q&A)

**Specs:**
- Feed: 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- Reels: 1080x1920px, 15-90 seconds
- Stories: 1080x1920px, 15 seconds each

---

### TikTok
**Frequency:** 4-5 posts per week

**Best Days/Times:**
- Tuesday-Thursday: 6-9am, 12-3pm, 7-11pm
- Weekend: 9am-12pm

**Content Types:**
- Educational hooks (10-30 sec)
- Transformation stories (30-60 sec)
- Trend participation (15-30 sec)
- Behind-the-scenes (15-45 sec)

**Specs:**
- Video: 1080x1920px (9:16)
- Length: 15-90 seconds (optimal: 21-34 sec)
- Captions: Always include for accessibility

---

### X (Twitter)
**Frequency:** 
- Threads: 2-3 per week
- Single tweets: Daily engagement
- Replies: Throughout the day

**Best Days/Times:**
- Monday-Friday: 8-10am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm
- Avoid: After 8pm, weekends

**Content Types:**
- Threads (6-12 tweets, educational)
- Single insights (punchy, quotable)
- Replies (community building)
- Retweets with commentary

**Character Limits:**
- Standard: 280 characters
- Threads: No limit on thread length

---

### YouTube
**Frequency:** 1 video per week (Fridays)

**Best Upload Times:**
- Friday: 2-4pm (for weekend viewing)
- Premiere schedule for engagement

**Content Types:**
- Tutorials (8-15 min)
- Deep dives (15-25 min)
- Case studies (10-20 min)
- Q&A sessions (15-30 min)

**Specs:**
- Video: 1920x1080px (16:9)
- Thumbnail: 1280x720px
- Description: 5,000 characters max

---

### Substack
**Frequency:**
- Essays: 1 per week (Sundays, 9am)
- Field Notes: 1 per week (Thursdays, 7am)
- Monthly Digest: 1st of each month

**Content Types:**
- **Essays (800-2,000 words):** Deep thought leadership
- **Field Notes (300-600 words):** Quick insights, observations
- **Monthly Digest:** Roundup of best content + exclusive insights

**Email Specs:**
- Subject line: 50 characters max
- Preview text: 100 characters
- Body: No strict limit (aim for 5-8 min read)

---

## Content Batching Schedule

### Monday (Production Day)
**9am-12pm:** Film video content
- 3-4 TikToks
- 2-3 Instagram Reels
- 1 YouTube video

**1pm-3pm:** Capture B-roll and photos
- Behind-the-scenes
- Product shots
- Lifestyle content

### Tuesday (Design Day)
**9am-12pm:** Create graphics
- 4-5 carousels
- Quote cards
- Infographics

**1pm-4pm:** Edit video content
- Add captions
- Apply brand templates
- Export for each platform

### Wednesday (Writing Day)
**9am-11am:** Write Substack content
- Draft essay
- Draft field notes

**11am-2pm:** Write social captions
- All posts for the week
- Adapt for each platform

**2pm-4pm:** Schedule posts
- Use scheduling tool
- Set optimal times
- Prepare hashtags

### Thursday (Engagement Day)
**Throughout day:** Community management
- Respond to comments
- Reply to DMs
- Engage with other accounts
- Network on LinkedIn

### Friday (Analysis Day)
**9am-11am:** Review analytics
- What performed well?
- What underperformed?
- Audience insights

**11am-1pm:** Plan next week
- Adjust strategy
- Brainstorm content ideas
- Create content calendar
