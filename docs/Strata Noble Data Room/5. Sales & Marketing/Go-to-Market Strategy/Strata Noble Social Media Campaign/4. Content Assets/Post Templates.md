# Strata Noble Content Post Templates

## Template 1: PROBLEM Post
**Hook:** Start with relatable pain point

**Structure:**
- Opening: "You're working 60-hour weeks but revenue is flat..."
- Elaborate: Why this problem matters now
- Insight: The hidden reason this happens
- Bridge: "But there's a better way..."
- CTA: Comment/engage/learn more

**Example:**
"Your business is stuck at $10K/month. Not because you're not working hard enough—you're exhausted. The real issue? You're building without systems. Every dollar earned costs you more time, more energy, more freedom. This is the entrepreneur's trap. Tomorrow, I'll show you how to break free. What's your biggest bottleneck right now?"

## Template 2: CHALLENGE Post
**Hook:** Raise the stakes

**Structure:**
- Opening: Acknowledge problem from previous post
- Escalation: "And it's about to get harder because..."
- Market reality: Competition, AI disruption, changing landscape
- Emotional impact: What happens if nothing changes
- Hope signal: "But you're not alone in this..."
- CTA: Share experience/continue thread

**Example:**
"Last week you told me: inconsistent revenue, no systems, endless firefighting. Here's what I didn't mention—it's about to get 10x harder. AI is eliminating entire business models. Your competitors are automating. The market is moving faster than ever. If you're stuck now, imagine next year. The gap between those who adapt and those who don't? It's becoming a chasm. Ready for the truth about what it takes to survive this?"

## Template 3: GUIDE Post

**Structure:**

- Empathy: "I know it feels impossible, but..."
- Preview: "Here's what we do differently..."
- CTA: Engage/DM/book call

"I've spent 15 years helping entrepreneurs break through the $10K ceiling. The ones who succeed? They don't work harder—they work differently. At Strata Noble, we combine habit science, AI strategy, and business systems to create sustainable growth. No hacks. No overnight success. Just proven frameworks that compound over time. DM me 'SYSTEMS' if you're ready to build differently."

## Template 4: TOOLS Post
**Hook:** Introduce the solution

**Structure:**
- Opening: "The tool that changed everything..."
- Specific solution: ACHIEVERY app / consulting service / framework
- How it works: 3 key features/benefits
- Why it's different: Unique mechanism
- Social proof: "Here's what happens when..."
- CTA: Try it/book it/learn more

**Example:**
"Meet ACHIEVERY—the app that builds business muscle memory. Here's how it works: 1) Daily micro-habits tailored to your business goals, 2) Progress tracking that shows real ROI, 3) AI-powered insights that adapt as you grow. It's not another task manager. It's a transformation engine. 30 days of consistent habits = 30 days closer to the business you actually want. Link in bio to start your first habit stack."

## Template 5: TRANSFORMATION Post
**Hook:** Show the end result

**Structure:**
- Opening: Customer success story or outcome
- Before state: Where they started
- Journey: What changed
- After state: Results (specific numbers/outcomes)
- Lesson: What this proves
- Invitation: "You could be next..."
- CTA: Apply/start/join

**Example:**
"Sarah was stuck at $8K/month for 2 years. Working 70 hours. Missing her kids' bedtimes. Six months with ACHIEVERY + our consulting? $45K/month. 30-hour weeks. She just booked a family vacation—her first in 3 years. The difference? She stopped 'hustling' and started building systems. Systems scale. You don't have to. Ready to write your transformation story? Drop a 🚀 below."

## Template 6: UNIQUE INSIGHTS Post
**Hook:** Surprise with rare information

**Structure:**
- Opening: "Here's what nobody's telling you about [topic]..."
- Data/insight: Specific, surprising fact or trend
- Analysis: Why this matters more than people think
- Prediction: Where this is heading
- Action: What to do about it now
- CTA: Save/share/discuss

**Example:**
"76% of businesses that integrated AI in 2024 saw revenue increase—but here's the part nobody mentions: 62% of those same businesses laid off their AI teams by month 8. Why? They automated the wrong things. AI isn't a replacement strategy—it's an amplification strategy. The founders winning right now? They're using AI to scale their strengths, not replace their weaknesses. Thread below on the 3 things you should NEVER automate 👇"

## Cadence Recommendation

**Weekly Posting Rhythm:**
- Monday: PROBLEM (start the week with recognition)
- Wednesday: CHALLENGE or GUIDE (build tension or offer path)
- Friday: TOOLS or TRANSFORMATION (provide solution or proof)
- Sunday: UNIQUE INSIGHTS (stand out with rare value)

**Platform Adaptations:**
- LinkedIn: Longer captions, professional tone, data-driven
- Instagram: Visual first, shorter hooks, story-driven
- TikTok: Fast-paced, trend-aware, personality-forward
- X: Punchy, thread-style, conversation-starting
- YouTube: Long-form deep dives, tutorial style
