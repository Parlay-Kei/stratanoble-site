## Platform-Specific Notes

**LinkedIn:** Post 3-4x per week, focus on Mon/Wed/Fri, professional tone, data-driven content

**Instagram:** Daily stories + 4 feed posts per week, visual-first, more personality

**TikTok:** 4-5x per week, trend-aware, educational + entertaining blend

**X:** 2-3 threads per week + daily engagement, punchy insights

**YouTube:** 1 long-form per week (Friday), evergreen tutorials

**Substack:** Sunday essay + Thursday field notes
