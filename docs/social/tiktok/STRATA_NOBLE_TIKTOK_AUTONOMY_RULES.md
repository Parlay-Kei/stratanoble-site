5. Script writing
6. Caption writing
7. Hashtag planning

9. Draft bank creation
10. Analytics review

Agent may prepare final publication packets:
1. Final scripts
2. Final captions
3. Final hashtags
4. Posting schedule
5. Shot list
6. Editing notes
7. Repurposing plan

Condition:
- Steve must approve first 14 posts before any publishing.

### Level 3: Full Posting Autonomy

Allowed only after all are true:
1. Steve approves first 14-post sample
2. Account access authority is confirmed
3. Brand Ops approves voice and positioning
4. QA Gatekeeper confirms compliance
5. Posting rules are documented in this file

Agent must stop and request Steve approval for:
1. Any direct revenue improvement claim
2. Any client naming
3. Any reference to Audrey, MAH, Direct Cuts, Q Suite, XLNT, PGV, or client work
4. Any paid promotion
5. Any controversial topic
6. Any new style using Steve face or voice
7. Any offer or price change
8. Any public claim about automation, AI, or proprietary systems

## Production System

### Weekly Production Checklist

1. Review previous 7 days analytics
2. Select top-performing hooks and topics
3. Build next 10 to 14 post slate
4. Draft scripts using required format
5. Build shot list and editing notes
6. Run QA checklist on every draft
7. Compile approval packet if in Level 2
8. Schedule posts after approval gate clears
9. Archive outputs in mission proof path

### Shot List Template

- Shot 1: Hook face-to-camera, 1 to 2 lines
- Shot 2: Supporting point with b-roll or on-screen text
- Shot 3: Example with screen, board, or text visual
- Shot 4: CTA delivery
- Coverage: alternate intro, close-up, and cutaway clip

### Editing Style Guide

- Fast opening with hook text in first second
- Clean cuts every 1 to 2 sentences
- Subtitles required
- Brand-consistent text overlays
- Avoid overdesigned effects
- Keep final length 18 to 45 seconds for core clips

### Caption Standards

- 1 to 3 short lines
- Lead with business pain or practical fix
- One clear CTA
- Plain language and operator tone

### Thumbnail and Text Overlay Standards

- High contrast text
- 4 to 8 words max on cover frame
- Problem or promise framing tied to operations
- Keep branding subtle and consistent

### Post Naming Convention

`TTK-YYYYMMDD-PILLAR-TOPIC-V1`

Example:
`TTK-20260512-BSD-INTAKE-TAX-V1`

## QA Gatekeeper Checklist

Every post must pass:
1. Voice fit to Strata Noble
2. Correct buyer targeting
3. Supportable claims only
4. No confidentiality leaks
5. No banned punctuation
6. No internal-only language
7. No lead-only positioning
8. Clear CTA
9. Specific business problem named
10. Reason to follow, comment, DM, or click

## Weekly Autonomous Operating Loop

1. Review performance data
2. Identify top hooks
3. Identify weak topics
4. Recommend next week topics
5. Produce scripts and packets
6. Prepare and queue posts
7. Submit approval packet until Level 3 is active
8. Publish within approved lanes once Level 3 is granted

## Operating Constraints

- No publishing until account authority and approval gate are complete
- No confidential client details in public content
- No unsupported financial claims
- No style drift from brand voice rules

## Steve Approval Packet Requirements

Packet must include:
1. Next 7 to 14 post scripts
2. Captions and hashtags
3. Video production notes
4. Risk flags by post
5. QA pass status per post
6. Requested approvals and publish windows
