## Objective

Build Strata Noble's TikTok into a client-acquisition and authority channel for:
- Solopreneurs trying to professionalize operations
- Service businesses under $500K with workflow issues
- Operators with weak intake, follow-up, and execution rhythm

Primary promise:
Strata Noble helps service businesses improve how work gets found, handled, followed up, delivered, tracked, and turned into repeatable revenue.

## TikTok Channel Strategy

Channel purpose:
- Turn "operational chaos pain" into educational content that earns trust
- Convert trust into profile visits, comments, DMs, and qualified inquiries
- Build repeat audience through practical, repeatable, useful clips

Content mix targets:
- 40% business systems breakdowns
- 20% solo operator reality
- 15% customer flow and follow-up
- 10% process improvement walkthroughs
- 10% offer education
- 5% founder POV

Cadence:
- Minimum: 1 post/day, 5 days/week
- Target: 2 posts/day, 5 days/week
- Weekend: optional lighter content, reactions, reposts

Weekly lane mapping:
- Monday: systems pain point
- Tuesday: solo workflow fix
- Wednesday: customer flow and follow-up
- Thursday: behind-the-build or offer education
- Friday: founder POV, mini audit, or hard truth
