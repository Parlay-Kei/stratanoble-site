# Business Segment Images Directory

This directory contains diverse business imagery aligned with the CONSOLIDATED_MESSAGING_FRAMEWORK.md for the WhatIsStrataNoble component.

## Required Images (Framework-Aligned)

Place the following images in this directory:

1. **local-practice.jpg** - Local Practices & Services (family medicine, dental, salons, gyms, repair shops)
2. **solo-consultant.jpg** - Solo and Micro-Firms (consultants, designers, bookkeepers, coaches)
3. **small-team.jpg** - Small Teams with No Real Back Office (agencies, boutiques, specialist shops)
4. **overwhelmed-owner.jpg** - The Overwhelmed Owner (solo business owner with visible workload)

## Image Guidelines (From Framework Section 10)

### ✅ MUST Include:

- **Mix of sectors**: Medical, salon, office, studio, shop
- **Mix of people**: Black, Brown, white, ages 20s-60s, different body types
- **Mix of styles**: Tattoos, suits, scrubs, hoodies
- **Realistic environments**: Small rooms, cluttered desks, whiteboards, calendars
- **Emotion range**: Focused, tired-but-engaged, relieved, collaborative

### ❌ NEVER Use:

- Corporate skyscraper stock photos
- Futuristic UI overlays
- All one industry in sequence
- Everyone under 35 and polished
- Excessive "stock photo smiles" - prefer "okay, we can handle this"

## Specific Image Requirements

### 1. Local Practice/Service (local-practice.jpg)
- Small clinic front desk OR salon chair area
- Person age 30-50, focused on schedule/client
- Natural lighting, realistic clutter
- Diverse representation

### 2. Solo Consultant (solo-consultant.jpg)
- Home office or small coworking space
- Person age 35-60, working on laptop
- Real environment (not staged corporate)
- Calm but engaged expression

### 3. Small Team (small-team.jpg)
- 2-3 people around laptop or whiteboard
- Modest office/backroom setting
- Mixed ages, diverse group
- Collaborative but realistic (not overly enthusiastic)

### 4. Overwhelmed Owner (overwhelmed-owner.jpg)
- Solo business owner with visible workload
- Papers, devices, calendar visible
- Tired-but-focused expression (not stressed to breaking point)
- Relatable, authentic moment

## Technical Specifications

- **Format**: JPG or WebP
- **Size**: 1200x800px minimum (3:2 aspect ratio)
- **Quality**: 85% compression (balance quality/performance)
- **Alt text**: Descriptive, includes business type and context

## Fallback Behavior

If images are not available, the component will display beautiful gradient backgrounds that match the brand colors. The component is fully functional without images, but images significantly enhance the messaging alignment with the framework.
