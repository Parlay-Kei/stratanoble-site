# CFO Financial Policy

**Owner**: CFO Agent
**Approved by**: Steve (Principal)
**Last Updated**: January 2026

---

## Purpose

Define financial guardrails that enable autonomous operation while protecting the company.

---

## Spending Authority

### CFO Approved

| Category | Single | Monthly |
|----------|--------|---------|
| Operating expenses | $500-$5,000 | <$500 |
| Vendor payments | Per contract | Per contract |
| Refunds | $100-$1,000 | - |
| Credits | <$50 | - |

### Principal Required

| Category | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| Any single expense | >$5,000 |
| Any recurring | >$500/month |
| Any refund | >$1,000 |
| Pricing changes | Any |
| New payment methods | Any |

---

## Revenue Policy

### Pricing Authority

- **CFO**: Enforce approved pricing
- **Growth Lead**: Discounts up to 10%
- **CFO + Growth Lead**: Discounts 10-25%
- **Principal**: Discounts >25%, pricing changes

### Revenue Recognition

- Recognize when service delivered
- Subscription: Recognize monthly
- One-time: Recognize on completion
- Follow GAAP principles

---

## Expense Policy

### Approval Requirements

| Amount | Approval | Documentation |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| <$500 | Auto | Receipt |
| $500-$5,000 | CFO | Quote + justification |
| >$5,000 | Principal | Full business case |

### Reimbursement

- Submit within 30 days
- Include receipts
- Follow expense categories
- CFO approves >$100

---

## Vendor Policy

### Evaluation Required

All vendors must have:
- [ ] Security review (Platform Ops)
- [ ] Contract review (Legal Ops)
- [ ] Budget approval (CFO)
- [ ] Business justification

### Contract Terms

- Prefer monthly/annual over multi-year
- Include termination clause
- No auto-renewal over $1K/mo
- Legal review for custom terms

---

## Payment Policy

### Accounts Payable

- Net 30 standard terms
- Pay on time (protect relationships)
- No early payment without discount

### Accounts Receivable

- Invoice promptly
- Follow up at 15, 30, 45 days
- Escalate at 60 days
- Collections at 90 days

---

## Cash Management

### Runway Target

- Maintain >12 months runway
- Alert at 9 months
- Escalate to Principal at 6 months

### Reserves

- Operating reserve: 2 months expenses
- Emergency fund: $[X]

### Payout Holds

### Commission Splits

## Reporting Requirements

### Weekly

- Burn rate
- Runway calculation
- Revenue vs target
- Notable expenses

### Monthly

- Full P&L
- Balance sheet
- Cash flow
- Unit economics (CAC, LTV)

### Quarterly

- Investor update (if applicable)
- Budget vs actual
- Forecast update

---

## Fraud Prevention

### Red Flags

- Unusual transaction patterns
- Duplicate invoices
- Missing documentation
- Circumventing approvals

### Response

1. Freeze suspicious activity
2. Investigate
3. Report to Legal Ops if confirmed
4. Remediate and document
