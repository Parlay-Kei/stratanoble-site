# Principal Operating Rules

**Owner**: Steve (Principal)
**Last Updated**: January 2026

---

## Role Definition

The Principal (Steve) is the human authority who:
- Sets strategic direction
- Approves irreversible decisions
- Manages investor relations
- Makes final calls on escalations

---

## Decision Framework

### Principal Decisions (Require My Approval)

| Category | Threshold |
|----------|-----------|
| **Financial** | Expenses >$5K, Recurring >$500/mo |
| **Legal** | Contracts >$50K, Policy changes, Litigation |
| **People** | Hiring, Firing, Raises |
| **Strategic** | Roadmap changes, New markets, Pivots |
| **Partnerships** | Binding commitments |

### Delegated to Agents

Everything else is delegated. Agents have authority within their domains per APPROVALS.md.

---

## Escalation Expectations

### When to Escalate to Me

1. **Irreversible decisions** above thresholds
2. **Unresolved conflicts** between agents
3. **Policy exceptions** requiring override
4. **P0 incidents** (for awareness)
5. **Legal/regulatory** issues

### How to Escalate

```
Subject: [ESCALATION] [Category] - [One-line summary]

Decision needed: [Clear ask]
Options: [A, B, C with trade-offs]
Recommendation: [Your suggestion]
Deadline: [When you need the decision]
```

### Response Times

| Severity | My Response |
|----------|-------------|
| P0/Critical | 15 minutes |
| P1/High | 4 hours |
| P2/Medium | 24 hours |
| P3/Low | 48 hours |

## Communication Preferences

### Do

- Lead with the decision needed
- Provide options with trade-offs
- Include your recommendation
- Be concise

### Don't

- Ask for permission on delegated matters
- Bring problems without solutions
- Escalate prematurely
- CC me on operational details

## Override Authority

I can override any agent decision, but I try not to. If agents are making good decisions within their authority, I stay out.

**If I override frequently**: Something is wrong with delegation or thresholds.

---

## Vacation/Unavailable Protocol

When I'm unavailable:
1. OCS has authority up to next threshold
2. P0 still reaches me via emergency channel
3. Defer non-urgent decisions
4. Document for my review on return
