# Legal Compliance Policy

**Owner**: Legal Ops Lead
**Approved by**: Steve (Principal)
**Last Updated**: January 2026

---

## Purpose

Define legal guardrails for compliant operation across all ANX ventures.

---

## Contract Policy

### Authority Levels

| Value | Approver | Review Required |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| <$10K | Legal Ops | Template only |
| $10K-$50K | Legal Ops + CFO | Full review |
| >$50K | Principal | Full review |

### Required Terms

All contracts must include:
- [ ] Termination for convenience
- [ ] Liability caps
- [ ] IP ownership clarity
- [ ] Data handling requirements
- [ ] Dispute resolution

### Prohibited Terms

Without Principal approval:
- Unlimited liability
- Exclusive arrangements >1 year
- Non-compete restrictions
- Automatic renewal >$1K/mo

## Privacy & Data Policy

### GDPR Compliance

| Requirement | Implementation |
|-------------|----------------|
| Lawful basis | Consent or contract |
| Data minimization | Collect only needed |
| Storage limitation | Retention policy |
| Right to access | DSR process |
| Right to erasure | Deletion workflow |

### CCPA Compliance

| Requirement | Implementation |
|-------------|----------------|
| Right to know | Privacy notice |
| Right to delete | Deletion workflow |
| Right to opt-out | Consent mechanism |
| Non-discrimination | Policy enforcement |

### Data Subject Requests

- Acknowledge within 24 hours
- Verify identity
- Complete within 30 days (GDPR) / 45 days (CCPA)
- Document for audit

## Employment & Contractor Policy

### Classification

| Factor | Employee | Contractor |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Control | Company directs | Independent |
| Tools | Company provides | Own tools |
| Schedule | Set hours | Flexible |
| Exclusivity | Often | Rarely |

### Required Agreements

- Employees: Offer letter, NDA, IP assignment
- Contractors: MSA, SOW, W-9

### Termination

- Always involve Legal Ops
- Document reason
- Follow applicable laws
- Collect company property

---

## Intellectual Property Policy

### Ownership

- All work product owned by ANX entity
- Employees sign IP assignment
- Contractors sign work-for-hire

### Third-Party IP

- License properly
- Track license terms
- Avoid GPL in proprietary code
- Document all dependencies

## Risk Assessment

### Risk Categories

| Level | Definition | Response |
|-------|------------|----------|
| Critical | Existential threat | Immediate Principal |
| High | Significant exposure | 24-hour response |
| Medium | Manageable risk | Plan mitigation |
| Low | Minor concern | Monitor |

### Risk Register

Maintain list of known risks with:
- Description
- Likelihood
- Impact
- Mitigation
- Owner

---

## Incident Response

### Legal Incidents

1. Contain (stop the harm)
2. Assess (understand impact)
3. Notify (Principal, insurers if needed)
4. Remediate (fix the issue)
5. Document (for learning and audit)

### Escalation Triggers

- Litigation threat
- Regulatory inquiry
- Data breach
- IP infringement claim
- Employment claim

## Legal Hold

When litigation is reasonably anticipated:
1. Issue hold notice
2. Preserve all relevant data
3. Suspend normal deletion
4. Document compliance
5. Release when resolved
