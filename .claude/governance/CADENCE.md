## Weekly Leadership Briefs

| Day | Brief | Owner | Notes |
|-----|-------|-------|-------|
| Monday | BI Brief | BI & Insights (or Product Lead if inactive) | Key metrics and anomalies |
| Wednesday | Growth Brief | Growth Lead | Acquisition, activation, retention |

## Minimal Status Rule

Only report what moved, what is blocked, and what needs a decision. Avoid extra narrative.
