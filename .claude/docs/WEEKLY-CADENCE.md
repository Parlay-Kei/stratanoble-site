## Monday: Metrics Brief

Start the week with clear visibility into performance and priorities.

### Time

### Participants

## Ad-Hoc Meetings

### Incident Response
- **Trigger**: P0 or P1 incident
- **Participants**: OCS, affected agents, QA Gatekeeper
- **Cadence**: Immediately, then hourly updates until resolved

### Executive Sync
- **Trigger**: Major decision or escalation
- **Participants**: Principal, relevant executives
- **Cadence**: As needed

### Venture Deep Dive
- **Trigger**: Quarterly or on request
- **Participants**: Venture GM, supporting agents, Principal
- **Cadence**: Monthly or quarterly
