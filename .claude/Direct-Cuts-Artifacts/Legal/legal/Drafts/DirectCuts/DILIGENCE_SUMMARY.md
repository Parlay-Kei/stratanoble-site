# DILIGENCE SUMMARY: DIRECT CUTS AGREEMENT PACKS

## 1. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY (IP) SECURITY

- **"Work Made for Hire":** The Senior Dev MSA explicitly categorizes work as
  "work made for hire," ensuring immediate Company ownership.
- **Fallback Assignment:** A "catch-all" assignment clause (Section 3.2)
  captures any IP that falls outside the statutory definition of work-for-hire.
- **Pre-Existing IP Schedule:** Exhibit A forces contractors to list prior
  inventions. Any IP _not_ listed is legally presumed to be new text generated
  for the Company.
- **Moral Rights Waiver:** Prevents developers from blocking future
  modifications to the code.

## 2. DELIVERY & PERFORMANCE RISK REDUCTION

- **Milestone-Based Payment:** The Senior Dev SOW ties payments to
  _deliverables_, not hours. Cash is preserved until code works.
- **Objective Acceptance:** "Acceptance Criteria" for each milestone reduces
  subjectivity and disputes about "done."
- **Proof Packs:** Payments are gated by "Proof Packs" (deployed links, commit
  hashes), creating an auditable chain of evidence for investors that the tech
  actually exists foundationally.

## 3. PLATFORM & SECURITY RISK

- **Access Controls:** The Security Addendum mandates MFA and "least privilege,"
  showing investors that security is an operational reality, not an
  afterthought.
- **Open Source Policy:** Explicit restrictions on "copyleft" (GPL) code prevent
  viral licensing that could devalue the proprietary codebase.
- **Termination Handoff:** The "48-hour rotation" clause ensures rapid severance
  of access, minimizing the window for disgruntled contractor damage.

## 4. COMPETITIVE INTEL & ADVISOR LIABILITY

- **Safe Harbor:** The Consultant Agreement explicitly limits advisor
  contributions to "public/personal" knowledge, insulating the Company from
  "tainted" trade secrets from competitors.
- **No Agency:** Clear language ("No authority to bind") prevents advisors from
  making deals on behalf of the Company.
- **measurable Impact:** The Ambassador SOW transforms "advisory" roles from
  vague coffee chats into measurable output (intros, memos, validation),
  justifying the equity/stipend on the cap table.
