# INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR SERVICES AGREEMENT

**EFFECTIVE DATE:** [DATE] **BETWEEN:**

**DIRECT CUTS, INC.** (the "Company"), a Delaware corporation with its principal
place of business at [ADDRESS]; and

**[CONTRACTOR NAME]** (the "Contractor"), an individual/entity with its
principal address at [ADDRESS].

## 1. ENGAGEMENT AND SERVICES

1.1 **Engagement.** The Company hereby engages the Contractor as an independent
contractor to provide the services described in one or more Statements of Work
("SOW") attached hereto (the "Services"). Contractor accepts such engagement.

1.2 **Independent Contractor Status.** Contractor is an independent contractor,
not an employee. Contractor is responsible for all taxes, including
self-employment taxes, and is not eligible for benefits (health insurance,
retirement, etc.) or workers' compensation. Contractor has no authority to bind
the Company to any contract or obligation.

1.3 **Non-Exclusivity.** This engagement is non-exclusive. Contractor may
perform services for others, provided such services do not create a conflict of
interest or result in the disclosure of Company Confidential Information.

## 2. COMPENSATION AND EXPENSES

2.1 **Fees.** Company shall pay Contractor the fees set forth in the applicable
SOW.

2.2 **Invoices.** Contractor shall submit invoices in accordance with the SOW.
Payment terms are Net 30 days from receipt of a correct invoice, unless
otherwise stated in the SOW.

2.3 **Expenses.** Company will reimburse reasonable, pre-approved out-of-pocket
expenses incurred in the performance of Services, subject to Company's expense
policy.

## 3. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

3.1 **Work Made for Hire.** To the fullest extent permitted by law, all "Work
Product" (defined as any code, documentation, designs, inventions, data, or
materials created by Contractor for Company) shall be deemed "work made for
hire" and owned exclusively by Company.

3.2 **Assignment.** To the extent any Work Product is not deemed a "work made
for hire," Contractor hereby irrevocably assigns to Company all right, title,
and interest in and to such Work Product, including all intellectual property
rights therein. Contractor agrees to execute any documents reasonably necessary
to perfect such assignment.

3.3 **Pre-Existing IP.** Contractor retains ownership of any IP developed prior
to this Agreement or independently of the Services ("Background IP"). If
Contractor incorporates Background IP into Work Product, Contractor grants
Company a perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free license to use,
modify, and distribute such Background IP as part of the Work Product.

3.4 **Moral Rights.** Contractor waives any "moral rights" in the Work Product
to the extent permitted by law.

## 4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 **Definition.** "Confidential Information" means any non-public information
disclosed by Company to Contractor, including but not limited to source code,
customer data, trade secrets, business plans, and financial information.

4.2 **Obligations.** Contractor agrees to: (a) hold Confidential Information in
strict confidence; (b) not disclose it to any third party without Company's
prior written consent; and (c) use it solely for the purpose of performing the
Services.

4.3 **Data Security.** Contractor shall handle all customer and barber data in
accordance with applicable laws and Company's security compliance requirements
as set forth in Exhibit B.

## 5. WARRANTIES AND INDEMNIFICATION

5.1 **Warranties.** Contractor represents and warrants that: (a) the Work
Product will be original and will not infringe any third-party rights; (b)
Contractor has the right to enter into this Agreement; and (c) the Services will
be performed in a professional manner.

5.2 **Indemnification.** Contractor agrees to indemnify and hold harmless
Company from any claims, damages, or expenses arising out of Contractor's breach
of this Agreement, negligence, or willful misconduct.

5.3 **Limitation of Liability.** EXCEPT FOR BREACHES OF CONFIDENTIALITY OR IP
OBLIGATIONS, NEITHER PARTY SHALL BE LIABLE FOR INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, OR
CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES. COMPANY'S TOTAL LIABILITY SHALL NOT EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES
PAID TO CONTRACTOR IN THE SIX (6) MONTHS PRIOR TO THE CLAIM.

## 6. TERM AND TERMINATION

6.1 **Term.** This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues until
terminated.

6.2 **Termination for Convenience.** Either party may terminate this Agreement
with [NUMBER] days' written notice.

6.3 **Termination for Cause.** Company may terminate immediately upon
Contractor's material breach.

6.4 **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Contractor shall immediately
stop work, return all Confidential Information and Work Product, and invoice for
Services performed up to the termination date.

## 7. RESTRICTIVE COVENANTS

7.1 **Non-Solicitation.** During the Term and for a period of twelve (12) months
thereafter, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly solicit or encourage any
employee or contractor of the Company to leave their engagement with the
Company.

7.2 **Non-Disparagement.** Contractor agrees not to make any false or
disparaging statements regarding the Company, its products, or its personnel.

7.3 **Publicity.** Contractor shall not use Company's name, logo, or reference
the engagement in press releases, case studies, or social media without
Company's prior written consent.

## 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS

8.1 **Governing Law.** This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State
of Nevada, without regard to its conflict of laws principles.

8.2 **Dispute Resolution.** Any disputes shall be resolved by binding
arbitration in [CITY], Nevada.

8.3 **Entire Agreement.** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement
between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements. Modifications must be
in writing.
