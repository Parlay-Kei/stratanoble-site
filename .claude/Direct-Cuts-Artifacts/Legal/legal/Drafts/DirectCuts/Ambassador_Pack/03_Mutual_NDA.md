## 1. PURPOSE

The parties wish to explore a potential business relationship (the "Purpose")
and may disclose Confidential Information to each other.

## 2. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by one
party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient"), whether orally or in writing,
that is designated as confidential or should reasonably be understood to be
confidential. This includes technical data, product roadmaps, customer lists,
and financial info.

## 3. OBLIGATIONS

Recipient agrees to: (a) Protect Confidential Information with the same degree
of care it uses for its own confidential info (but no less than reasonable
care). (b) Not disclose Confidential Information to any third party (except
employees/contractors with a "need to know" who are bound by confidentiality).
(c) Use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose.

## 4. EXCLUSIONS

Obligations do not apply to information that: (a) Is or becomes public through
no fault of Recipient. (b) Was known to Recipient prior to disclosure. (c) Is
rightfully obtained from a third party without restriction. (d) Is independently
developed by Recipient.

## 5. TERM

This Agreement controls information disclosed within one (1) year of the
Effective Date. The duty of confidentiality survives for three (3) years from
the date of disclosure (Trade Secrets survive indefinitely).
