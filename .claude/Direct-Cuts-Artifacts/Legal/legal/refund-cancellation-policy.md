# Refund and Cancellation Policy

**Direct Cuts LLC**

**Last Updated: December 31, 2024**

**Effective Date: December 31, 2024**

---

## Overview

This Refund and Cancellation Policy explains the terms under which bookings may be canceled and refunds may be issued on the Direct Cuts platform. This policy applies to both Customers and Barbers.

Direct Cuts facilitates transactions between Customers and independent Barbers. While we strive to ensure fair outcomes for all parties, specific circumstances may affect refund eligibility.

---

## 1. Customer Cancellation Policy

### 1.1 Cancellation Windows

**Free Cancellation (Full Refund):**
- Cancel more than **24 hours** before your scheduled appointment time
- You will receive a full refund to your original payment method

**Late Cancellation (Partial Refund):**
- Cancel between **2 and 24 hours** before your scheduled appointment time
- You may receive a **50% refund**, depending on the Barber's cancellation policy
- The remaining 50% compensates the Barber for the reserved time slot

**Very Late Cancellation (No Refund):**
- Cancel less than **2 hours** before your scheduled appointment time
- No refund will be issued
- The full payment will be released to the Barber

### 1.2 How to Cancel

You can cancel a booking through the Direct Cuts app:
1. Go to the **Bookings** tab
2. Select the appointment you wish to cancel
3. Tap **Cancel Booking**
4. Confirm the cancellation

Cancellations are processed immediately, and applicable refunds are initiated automatically.

### 1.3 Cancellation by Appointment Type

**In-Shop Appointments:**
- Standard cancellation windows apply as described above

**Mobile Service Appointments:**
- Standard cancellation windows apply
- If the Barber has already begun traveling to your location, a travel fee may be retained from your refund

---

## 2. Barber Cancellation Policy

### 2.1 Barber-Initiated Cancellations

When a Barber cancels an appointment:
- The Customer receives a **full refund** regardless of timing
- The refund is processed automatically
- The Customer is notified immediately via push notification and email

### 2.2 Excessive Barber Cancellations

Barbers who cancel excessively may face:
- Warning notifications
- Reduced visibility in search results
- Temporary suspension
- Account termination for repeated violations

We understand emergencies happen, but consistent reliability is essential for maintaining platform trust.

### 2.3 Barber No-Show

If a Barber fails to appear for a confirmed appointment:
- The Customer receives a **full refund**
- The Barber may face account penalties
- Please refer to our No-Show Policy for details

---

## 3. Refund Processing

### 3.1 Refund Timeline

- **Initiation:** Refunds are initiated within 1-2 business days of cancellation
- **Processing:** Refunds typically appear on your statement within 5-10 business days
- **Timing varies:** Actual timing depends on your payment method and financial institution

### 3.2 Refund Method

- Refunds are issued to the **original payment method** used for the booking
- If the original payment method is no longer valid, contact support@direct-cuts.com

### 3.3 Partial Refunds

Partial refunds may apply in the following situations:
- Late cancellations (as described in Section 1.1)
- Partial service completion (if you leave mid-service)
- Service modifications after booking
- Travel fee retention for mobile cancellations

---

## 4. Non-Refundable Situations

### 4.1 When Refunds Are Not Available

Refunds are generally **not** provided in the following circumstances:

- **No-shows by Customer:** If you fail to appear for your appointment without canceling
- **Very late cancellations:** Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment
- **Service dissatisfaction without dispute:** If you received the service but are unsatisfied, please file a dispute rather than requesting a refund
- **Completed services:** Services that have been fully performed
- **Violation of Terms:** Bookings canceled due to Customer violation of our Terms of Service

### 4.2 Change of Mind

We understand plans change. However, once services have begun or passed the cancellation window, "change of mind" is not a valid reason for a refund.

---

## 5. Service-Related Issues

### 5.1 Incomplete or Unsatisfactory Service

If you believe the service you received was incomplete, significantly different from what was described, or unsatisfactory:

1. **Contact the Barber first:** Many issues can be resolved directly
2. **File a dispute:** If you cannot resolve the issue, submit a dispute through the app
3. **We will investigate:** Our team will review the circumstances and make a determination

Please refer to our Dispute Policy for the full dispute resolution process.

### 5.2 Safety Concerns

If you have safety concerns during an appointment, you may:
- Leave the appointment immediately
- Contact us at support@direct-cuts.com
- File a report through the app

Safety-related cancellations will be reviewed individually, and appropriate refunds may be issued.

---

## 6. Special Circumstances

### 6.1 Emergency Cancellations

We understand that emergencies happen. If you need to cancel due to:
- Medical emergency
- Family emergency
- Natural disaster
- Other extraordinary circumstances

Please contact support@direct-cuts.com with relevant documentation. We will review requests on a case-by-case basis and may waive cancellation fees in genuine emergency situations.

### 6.2 Platform Technical Issues

If you are unable to cancel through the app due to technical issues:
- Email support@direct-cuts.com immediately with your booking details
- Include screenshots if possible
- We will honor the cancellation time based on when you contacted us

### 6.3 Booking Errors

If you made a booking in error (wrong time, wrong service, wrong Barber):
- Cancel as soon as you realize the error
- The standard cancellation windows apply
- Contact support if you believe special circumstances warrant an exception

---

## 7. Modifications vs. Cancellations

### 7.1 Rescheduling

Rescheduling is treated as a new booking:
- Cancel the existing appointment (standard policies apply)
- Create a new booking for your preferred time
- No additional fees beyond any applicable cancellation fees

### 7.2 Service Modifications

To modify services within an existing booking:
- Contact the Barber directly through in-app messaging
- The Barber may accept or decline modifications
- Price adjustments will be reflected accordingly

---

## 8. Platform Fees

### 8.1 Fee Handling on Cancellations

**Customer Cancellation (Full Refund):**
- Full payment is refunded to Customer
- No platform fee is charged

**Late Cancellation (Partial Refund):**
- Platform fee is deducted from the portion retained by the Barber
- Standard 15% platform fee applies

**Barber Cancellation:**
- Full payment is refunded to Customer
- No platform fee is charged to either party
