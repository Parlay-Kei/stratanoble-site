# No-Show Policy

**Direct Cuts LLC**

**Last Updated: December 31, 2024**

**Effective Date: December 31, 2024**

---

## Overview

This No-Show Policy explains the consequences and procedures when a Customer or Barber fails to appear for a confirmed appointment. No-shows waste time and resources for all parties, and consistent attendance is essential for a healthy marketplace.

---

## 1. Definition of No-Show

### 1.1 Customer No-Show

A Customer is considered a no-show when:
- The Customer fails to arrive at the appointment location
- The Customer arrives more than **15 minutes late** without prior communication
- The Customer cannot be reached after reasonable attempts by the Barber
- For mobile services: the Customer is not present or reachable at the specified address

### 1.2 Barber No-Show

A Barber is considered a no-show when:
- The Barber fails to be present at the appointment location
- The Barber is more than **15 minutes late** without prior communication to the Customer
- The Barber cannot be reached after reasonable attempts
- For mobile services: the Barber fails to arrive at the Customer's location

### 1.3 Grace Period

A **15-minute grace period** applies to all appointments. During this time:
- The arriving party should attempt to contact the other party
- No penalties apply if the late party arrives within the grace period
- After 15 minutes, the present party may mark the appointment as a no-show

---

## 2. Customer No-Show Consequences

### 2.1 Financial Consequences

**First No-Show:**
- Full booking amount is charged to the Customer
- 100% of the service fee is paid to the Barber
- Platform fee is deducted per standard terms

**Subsequent No-Shows:**
- Full booking amount is charged
- Warning notification is sent
- Account review may be triggered

### 2.2 Account Consequences

| No-Shows | Consequence |
|----------|-------------|
| 1st | Payment forfeit + warning |
| 2nd | Payment forfeit + account review |
| 3rd within 6 months | Payment forfeit + possible suspension |
| 4+ within 6 months | Account termination consideration |

### 2.3 No-Show Fee Structure

The no-show fee is equal to the **full booking amount** including:
- All selected services
- Any mobile service fees
- Travel fees (if applicable)

This compensates the Barber for the reserved time slot that cannot be recovered.

---

## 3. Barber No-Show Consequences

### 3.1 Customer Compensation

When a Barber no-shows:
- Customer receives a **full refund** automatically
- Refund is processed within 1-2 business days
- Customer may leave a review (marked as verified no-show)

### 3.2 Barber Account Consequences

| No-Shows | Consequence |
|----------|-------------|
| 1st | Full refund to Customer + warning |
| 2nd within 3 months | Reduced search visibility for 7 days |
| 3rd within 6 months | Account suspension for 14 days |
| 4+ within 6 months | Account termination |

### 3.3 Impact on Barber Standing

Barber no-shows negatively affect:
- Search ranking and visibility
- Overall platform rating
- Eligibility for featured placement
- Trust indicators on profile

---

## 4. Reporting a No-Show

### 4.1 How to Report (Customer Reporting Barber)

1. Wait until **15 minutes past** the appointment time
2. Open the appointment in the **Bookings** tab
3. Tap **Report No-Show**
4. Confirm the report

Your refund will be processed automatically.

### 4.2 How to Report (Barber Reporting Customer)

1. Wait until **15 minutes past** the appointment time
2. Open the appointment in your **Schedule** tab
3. Tap **Mark as No-Show**
4. Confirm the report

Payment will be released to you per standard terms.

### 4.3 Verification

We may verify no-show reports through:
- Location data (if available and consented)
- Communication records
- Pattern analysis
- Follow-up with both parties

False no-show reports are a serious violation and may result in account termination.

## 5. Exceptions and Appeals

### 5.1 Valid Exceptions

No-show penalties may be waived in cases of:
- Documented medical emergency
- Natural disaster or severe weather
- Platform technical issues preventing cancellation
- Verifiable extenuating circumstances

### 5.2 How to Appeal

If you believe a no-show was recorded in error or due to exceptional circumstances:

1. **Contact us within 48 hours** of the appointment
2. Email support@direct-cuts.com with subject "No-Show Appeal"
3. Include:
   - Booking reference number
   - Explanation of circumstances
   - Supporting documentation (if applicable)

### 5.3 Appeal Timeline

- Appeals are reviewed within **3-5 business days**
- Decisions are communicated via email
- Refunds or payment adjustments are processed within 2 business days of decision

---

## 6. Late Arrivals (Not No-Shows)

### 6.1 Within Grace Period (0-15 Minutes Late)

- No penalty applies
- Service proceeds as normal
- The service may need to be shortened to accommodate subsequent appointments

### 6.2 Beyond Grace Period (15+ Minutes Late)

**Customer Late:**
- The Barber may choose to:
  - Still provide service (potentially shortened)
  - Mark as no-show and receive payment
  - Reschedule (at their discretion)

**Barber Late:**
- The Customer may choose to:
  - Wait for the Barber
  - Cancel for a full refund
  - Accept a partial service

### 6.3 Communication is Key

If you know you will be late:
- Contact the other party immediately through the app
- Communicate your estimated arrival time
- Confirm they can still accommodate you

Good communication can prevent no-show situations.

---

## 7. Mobile Service Special Considerations

### 7.1 Customer Responsibilities

For mobile services, Customers must:
- Be present at the specified address
- Ensure access to the location
- Have the service area prepared
- Be reachable by phone

### 7.2 Address Issues

If the Barber cannot access the location due to:
- Incorrect address
- No response at the door
- Building access issues
- Unsafe conditions

This may be treated as a Customer no-show.

### 7.3 Barber Travel Time

For mobile services:
- The Barber's travel time is factored into appointment timing
- If a Customer cancels after the Barber has begun traveling, the travel fee may still apply
- If a Customer no-shows a mobile appointment, the full amount including travel fees is charged

---

## 8. Repeated Patterns

### 8.1 Pattern Detection

Our system monitors for patterns including:
- Multiple no-shows in a short period
- Repeated late cancellations
- Frequent disputes following appointments
- Gaming of cancellation windows

### 8.2 Consequences of Patterns

Users showing problematic patterns may face:
- Increased scrutiny on future bookings
- Required deposits or prepayment
- Restricted booking capabilities
- Account suspension or termination

---

## 9. Best Practices to Avoid No-Shows

### 9.1 For Customers

- Set calendar reminders for your appointment
- Confirm the address and time the day before
- Enable push notifications for booking reminders
- If plans change, cancel early to avoid fees
- Keep your phone nearby on appointment day

### 9.2 For Barbers

- Send appointment reminders to Customers
- Confirm appointments the day before
- Communicate any delays immediately
- Keep your schedule accurate and updated
- Build buffer time between appointments
