**Last Updated: December 31, 2024**

## Overview

This Dispute Policy outlines the process for resolving disputes between Customers and Barbers on the Direct Cuts platform. We are committed to fair and timely resolution of issues that may arise during the use of our services.

Direct Cuts acts as a neutral facilitator in disputes. While we strive for fair outcomes, we encourage users to first attempt direct resolution with the other party.

---

## 1. What Constitutes a Dispute

### 1.1 Valid Dispute Reasons

You may file a dispute for the following reasons:

**Service Quality Issues:**
- Service significantly different from what was described or shown
- Incomplete service (left before finishing without Customer consent)
- Unprofessional conduct during the appointment

**Booking Issues:**
- Barber no-show without cancellation
- Service provided by a different person than the booked Barber
- Significant delay (more than 30 minutes) without communication

**Payment Issues:**
- Charged incorrectly or more than the agreed price
- Duplicate charges
- Charges for canceled appointments that should have been refunded

**Safety Concerns:**
- Unsafe or unsanitary conditions
- Harassment or inappropriate behavior
- Violation of health and safety standards

### 1.2 Invalid Dispute Reasons

The following are generally **not** valid dispute reasons:
- Change of mind after service completion
- Minor dissatisfaction with styling preferences (subjective)
- Issues that were not raised during or immediately after the appointment
- Price disputes when the price was clearly displayed before booking
- Matters outside the scope of the Direct Cuts platform

---

## 2. Before Filing a Dispute

### 2.1 Direct Communication First

Before filing a formal dispute, we strongly encourage you to:

1. **Contact the other party directly** through in-app messaging
2. **Explain the issue clearly** and what resolution you are seeking
3. **Allow reasonable time** (24-48 hours) for a response
4. **Attempt to reach an agreement** on a fair resolution

Many issues can be resolved quickly through direct communication without formal intervention.

### 2.2 Document the Issue

Before filing a dispute, gather:
- Booking confirmation details
- Photos or screenshots (if applicable)
- Messages with the other party
- Clear description of what happened
- Receipt of what you paid

---

## 3. How to File a Dispute

### 3.1 Filing Deadline

Disputes must be filed within **7 days** of the appointment date. After 7 days, disputes may not be considered except in exceptional circumstances.

### 3.2 Filing Process

**Step 1: Open the Booking**
- Go to the **Bookings** tab in the app
- Find the relevant appointment
- Tap **Report Issue** or **File Dispute**

**Step 2: Select Dispute Category**
- Choose the category that best describes your issue
- This helps us route your dispute appropriately

**Step 3: Describe the Issue**
- Provide a clear, factual description of what happened
- Include relevant dates, times, and details
- Explain what resolution you are seeking

**Step 4: Submit Evidence**
- Upload any photos, screenshots, or documentation
- Include message history if relevant

**Step 5: Submit the Dispute**
- Review your submission
- Submit for review

### 3.3 Confirmation

You will receive:
- Confirmation email/notification that your dispute was received
- A dispute reference number for tracking
- Estimated timeline for resolution

---

## 4. Dispute Resolution Process

### 4.1 Initial Review (1-2 Business Days)

Our team will:
- Acknowledge receipt of your dispute
- Review the initial submission
- Contact you if additional information is needed
- Notify the other party that a dispute has been filed

### 4.2 Investigation (3-5 Business Days)

During investigation, we may:
- Request additional information from both parties
- Review booking and transaction records
- Review message history between parties
- Examine any evidence submitted
- Consider the history and standing of both accounts

### 4.3 Resolution (Within 7-10 Business Days Total)

After investigation, we will:
- Make a determination based on the evidence
- Notify both parties of the decision
- Process any applicable refunds or adjustments
- Document the resolution

### 4.4 Timeline Summary

| Stage | Typical Timeline |
|-------|-----------------|
| Acknowledgment | Within 24 hours |
| Initial Review | 1-2 business days |
| Investigation | 3-5 business days |
| Final Resolution | 7-10 business days total |

*Complex disputes may take longer. We will keep you informed of any delays.*

---

## 5. Possible Resolutions

### 5.1 For Customers

Depending on the circumstances, resolutions may include:
- **Full refund** for significant service failures or no-shows
- **Partial refund** for partial service issues
- **Service credit** for future bookings
- **No action** if the dispute is not substantiated

### 5.2 For Barbers

Depending on the circumstances, resolutions may include:
- **Full payment release** if the dispute is unfounded
- **Partial payment** if there were legitimate service issues
- **No payment** for verified no-shows or significant failures

### 5.3 Account Actions

In addition to financial resolutions, we may take account actions including:
- Formal warnings
- Required acknowledgment of policies
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent account termination (for serious or repeated violations)

---

## 6. Payment Holds During Disputes

### 6.1 For Customers

If you file a dispute, your original payment remains on hold until the dispute is resolved. No additional charges will be made during the dispute process.

### 6.2 For Barbers

When a dispute is filed:
- Payment for the disputed booking will be held
- This does not affect payments for other completed bookings
- Funds will be released according to the dispute resolution

---

## 7. Appeals

### 7.1 Eligibility

You may appeal a dispute decision if:
- You have new evidence not previously considered
- You believe there was an error in the review process
- The decision was based on incorrect information

### 7.2 How to Appeal

- **Timeline:** Appeals must be submitted within **5 days** of the decision
- **Method:** Email support@direct-cuts.com with subject line "Dispute Appeal - [Reference Number]"
- **Content:** Clearly explain why you believe the decision should be reconsidered

### 7.3 Appeal Review

Appeals are reviewed by a different team member than the original dispute. Appeal decisions are final.

---

## 8. Dispute Limits and Abuse

### 8.1 Frivolous Disputes

Users who file frivolous, fraudulent, or bad-faith disputes may face:
- Warnings
- Denial of future disputes
- Account suspension or termination

### 8.2 Patterns of Disputes

We monitor dispute patterns. Accounts with excessive disputes (as either filer or subject) may be reviewed for:
- Quality issues
- Policy compliance
- Continued platform access

---

## 9. Chargebacks

### 9.1 What is a Chargeback?

A chargeback is when you dispute a charge directly with your bank or credit card company instead of through our platform.

### 9.2 Our Position on Chargebacks

We strongly encourage you to resolve issues through our dispute process rather than chargebacks. Chargebacks:
- Take longer to resolve (weeks to months)
- Remove our ability to mediate fairly
- May result in account suspension pending investigation
- Incur fees that affect the resolution amount

### 9.3 Chargeback Consequences

Filing a chargeback before using our dispute process may result in:
- Immediate account suspension
- Inability to continue using the platform until resolved
- Permanent account termination for fraudulent chargebacks

## 10. Specific Dispute Types

### 10.1 No-Show Disputes

**Customer No-Show:**
- Barber may file a dispute to claim the booking fee
- Please refer to our No-Show Policy

**Barber No-Show:**
- Customer receives an automatic full refund
- File a dispute if the refund is not processed

### 10.2 Service Quality Disputes

For service quality issues:
- Be specific about what was different from expectations
- Photos are extremely helpful
- Compare to the service description and Barber's portfolio

### 10.3 Safety Disputes

Safety-related disputes are prioritized:
- Contact us immediately if you feel unsafe
- We may request additional documentation
- Serious safety issues may be reported to authorities

## 13. Limitations

### 13.1 Direct Cuts Role

Direct Cuts is a technology platform that facilitates connections between Customers and independent Barbers. We are not:
- A party to the service transaction
- Responsible for the quality of barbering services
- An arbiter of subjective quality disputes

### 13.2 Scope

Our dispute process covers issues directly related to bookings and transactions on our platform. Issues outside this scope may need to be resolved through other means.
