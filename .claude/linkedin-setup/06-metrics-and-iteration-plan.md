# Metrics and Iteration Plan - LinkedIn Performance Tracking

## Overview

This document provides a lightweight framework for tracking LinkedIn performance, identifying what works, and iterating on content and engagement strategy.

**Review Cadence:** Weekly reviews + Monthly deep dives
**Time Investment:** 15-20 minutes per week for tracking, 30-45 minutes per month for analysis

---

## Part 1: Weekly Tracking Metrics

**Content Performance:**
- [ ] Post impressions (total views across all posts this week)
- [ ] Engagement rate (total engagements / total impressions x 100)

- [ ] Click-through rate (if using links)

**Audience Growth:**
- [ ] New followers this week
- [ ] New connections this week
- [ ] Connection acceptance rate (accepted / sent x 100)
- [ ] Profile views this week

**Engagement Activity:**
- [ ] Comments you left on others' posts
- [ ] Connection requests sent
- [ ] DMs sent (warm outreach)
- [ ] Responses to comments on your posts

---

### Company Page Metrics (Track Weekly)

## Part 3: Accessing LinkedIn Analytics

### Personal Profile Analytics

**How to Access:**
1. Go to your LinkedIn profile
2. Click "Analytics" (below your profile introduction)
3. View tabs: "Post impressions", "Profile views", "Search appearances"

**What to Look For:**
- **Post impressions:** Total views of your content. Track week-over-week growth.
- **Profile views:** Spikes indicate interest from your posts or engagement activity.
- **Search appearances:** Shows how often you appear in LinkedIn searches. Increases as you post more.

**Individual Post Analytics:**
1. Go to any published post
2. Click "View analytics" below the post
3. Review: Impressions, Reactions, Comments, Shares, Engagement rate

---

### Company Page Analytics

**How to Access:**
1. Go to your company page (Strata Noble Consulting)
2. Click "Analytics" (admin view only)
3. View tabs: "Visitors", "Updates", "Followers", "Competitors" (optional)

**What to Look For:**
- **Updates:** Post performance (impressions, engagement, CTR)
- **Visitors:** Page views, unique visitors, demographics
- **Followers:** Growth trends, follower demographics, how people found you

## Part 5: Success Benchmarks

### Weeks 1-4 (Establishment Phase)

**Goals:**
- Publish 3 posts per week consistently
- Gain 10-20 new connections
- Achieve 1-2% engagement rate on posts
- Get at least 50-100 impressions per post

**Success Indicators:**
- You are showing up consistently
- A few people are engaging with your content
- Profile looks complete and professional

---

### Weeks 5-8 (Growth Phase)

**Goals:**
- Increase post impressions to 200-300 per post
- Achieve 2-3% engagement rate
- Gain 20-30 new connections
- Receive 1-2 inbound inquiries or meaningful DMs

**Success Indicators:**
- Content is starting to resonate
- People are commenting and asking questions
- Profile views are increasing
- You are having real conversations in comments or DMs

---

### Weeks 9-12 (Momentum Phase)

**Goals:**
- Achieve 300-500+ impressions per post
- Maintain 3-5% engagement rate
- Gain 30-50 new connections
- Receive 3-5 inbound inquiries or collaboration opportunities

**Success Indicators:**
- You have a regular audience
- Posts are shared beyond your immediate network
- People are reaching out proactively
- You are being seen as a credible voice in your niche

---

### Months 4-6 (Authority Phase)

**Goals:**
- Achieve 500-1000+ impressions per post
- Maintain 5%+ engagement rate
- Gain 50-100 new connections per month
- Convert 1-2 LinkedIn conversations into client calls or projects

**Success Indicators:**
- Your content is referenced or shared by others
- You are getting tagged in relevant conversations
- Inbound inquiries are qualified and aligned with your ICP
- LinkedIn is a consistent source of leads or partnerships

## Part 6: Iteration Framework

### If Posts Are Not Getting Impressions:

**Possible Causes:**
1. Posting at low-traffic times
2. Content is too niche or not relevant to your network
3. No engagement in the first hour (algorithm deprioritizes)
4. Hashtags are too broad or irrelevant

**Action Steps:**
- Test different posting times (morning vs. midday vs. evening)
- Broaden topics slightly to appeal to a wider audience within your niche
- Engage with others' content before posting (algorithm rewards active users)
- Refine hashtag strategy (use 3-5 relevant, moderately popular hashtags)

---

### If Posts Are Getting Impressions But No Engagement:

**Possible Causes:**
1. Content is informational but not conversation-starting
2. No clear CTA or question
3. Format is hard to read (wall of text, no line breaks)
4. Topic is interesting but not actionable

**Action Steps:**
- Add a question or CTA at the end of each post
- Use shorter paragraphs and line breaks for readability
- Shift from pure advice to case stories or contrarian takes (more engaging)
- Make content actionable (give people something to try or respond to)

---

### If You Are Getting Engagement But No Inbound Inquiries:

**Possible Causes:**
1. CTA is not clear or compelling
2. Profile does not clearly explain what you offer
3. Featured section is empty or outdated
4. Content is helpful but not positioned as a service

**Action Steps:**
- Strengthen CTAs (make it clear how people can work with you)
- Update About section to include a clear service offering
- Add Featured items (case studies, service overview, audit offer)
- Occasionally include service-related posts (like the AI Automation Audit post)

---

### If Connection Requests Are Not Being Accepted:

**Possible Causes:**
1. Generic or no connection note
2. Profile does not clearly show your value or credibility
3. Sending requests to people outside your target audience

**Action Steps:**
- Always include a personalized connection note
- Optimize profile (complete About section, professional photo, clear headline)
- Target people who have engaged with your content or are in your ICP
