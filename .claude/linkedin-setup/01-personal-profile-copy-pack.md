### Featured Item 3: Proof Post - Case Story

**Title:** How One Operator Saved 12 Hours Per Week

**Content:**

A small contracting business was losing deals because follow-up happened late or not at all. The owner spent evenings manually reviewing leads, sending status emails, and updating spreadsheets.

**The Problem:**
- Leads came from multiple channels (web form, text, email, referral calls)
- No central tracking system
- Follow-up was manual and inconsistent
- Owner spent 12+ hours/week on admin instead of sales or delivery

**The Solution:**
- Built a simple intake router that normalized all leads into one system
- Created automated follow-up sequences based on lead status
- Set up status tracking with notifications for overdue items
- Designed a weekly dashboard showing pipeline movement

**The Result:**
- Follow-up response time dropped from 24+ hours to under 2 hours
- Owner reclaimed 12 hours per week for revenue work
- Conversion rate improved by 18% in the first 60 days
- System runs with minimal oversight

**The Approach:**
We did not rip out existing tools. We connected what was already there and automated the gaps. Implementation took 3 weeks. The owner was hands-on for training but not for setup.

If your follow-up is falling through the cracks, this is fixable. Email steve.hubbard@stratanoble.com to discuss your workflow.
