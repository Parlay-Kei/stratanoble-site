## Critical Reminders

### DO:
- Post consistently (3x per week minimum)
- Engage authentically (15-20 minutes daily)
- Respond to all comments within 24 hours
- Personalize every connection request and DM
- Track metrics weekly and adjust based on data
- Focus on building relationships, not pitching services

### DO NOT:
- Use LinkedIn scraping tools or unofficial APIs
- Use browser automation (Selenium, Puppeteer, etc.)
- Send generic connection requests or DMs
- Copy-paste the same comment on multiple posts
- Post and ghost (engagement is critical for reach)
- Over-promote services (80% value, 20% promotion)

---

## Risk Mitigation

### If Posts Are Not Getting Impressions:
- Test different posting times
- Engage with others' content before posting
- Refine hashtag strategy
- Broaden topics slightly to appeal to wider audience

### If Engagement Is Low:
- Add clear CTA or question at end of posts
- Use shorter paragraphs and line breaks for readability
- Shift to more engaging formats (case stories, contrarian takes)
- Make content actionable

### If Connection Requests Are Rejected:
- Always include personalized note
- Only connect with people in your ICP or who engage with your content
- Optimize profile to clearly show value

### If Time Becomes Constrained:
- Reduce posting frequency to 2x per week (still effective)
- Focus engagement time on highest-value accounts
- Use LinkedIn native scheduling to batch content prep
- Prioritize responding to comments on your posts over new engagement
