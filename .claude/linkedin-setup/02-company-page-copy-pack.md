## Starter Posts (3 Additional Posts)

### Post 2: Problem/Solution Format

```
Small operators lose deals in the follow-up gap.

The problem is not leads. It is what happens after the lead comes in.

Intake happens across multiple channels: web form, text message, email, phone call, referral. Each lead lands in a different place. The operator manually checks each channel, copies information into a spreadsheet or CRM, and tries to remember who needs follow-up and when.

By the time follow-up happens, the lead has moved on or the moment has passed.

The fix is not working harder. It is routing and automation.

A simple intake router normalizes all leads into one system. Automated follow-up sequences trigger based on status and timeline. Notifications flag overdue items before they become lost opportunities.

One client reduced follow-up response time from 24+ hours to under 2 hours and reclaimed 12 hours per week. Conversion improved by 18% in 60 days.

If your follow-up is falling through the cracks, this is fixable.

Contact: steve.hubbard@stratanoble.com
```

### Post 3: Insight/Advice Format

```
The best automation is the one your team will actually use.

Complexity kills adoption. If the system requires 12 clicks, a manual export, and a prayer, it will not get used consistently.

When designing workflows for small operators, we optimize for three things:

1. Low friction: The system should fit the way people already work, not force a new process that adds cognitive load.

2. Visibility: Everyone should be able to see status, next steps, and what is overdue without asking.

3. Maintainability: If the system breaks when one person is out sick, it is too fragile.

Most small businesses do not need enterprise-grade tooling. They need their existing tools connected properly and their repetitive work automated.

Start with the biggest time drain. Automate that first. Then move to the next bottleneck.

Strata Noble Consulting builds practical automations for solo and small businesses. We focus on intake, follow-up, scheduling, and reporting.

Contact: steve.hubbard@stratanoble.com
```
